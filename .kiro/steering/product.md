# Product Overview

## What is Focus Dashboard?

Focus Dashboard is a simple productivity dashboard designed to help students stay focused and organized. It combines essential productivity tools in one clean interface:

- **Personalized Greeting**: Display current time and a contextual greeting based on the time of day
- **Focus Timer**: Pomodoro-style timer to structure work sessions
- **To-Do List**: Simple task management to track daily goals
- **Quick Links**: Fast access to frequently used websites
- **Light/Dark Mode**: Visual comfort with theme switching

## Target User

Students who need a lightweight, distraction-free tool to manage their study sessions, track tasks, and quickly access their most-used resources without the complexity of heavy productivity apps.

## Core Philosophy

Keep it simple, fast, and focused. No distractions, no complexity, no learning curve. Just open and start working.
